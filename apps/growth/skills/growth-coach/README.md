# growth-coach

**The coach behind Growth.** It holds the rules Growth coaches by: diagnose before advising, fix the earliest broken stage of your job search first, plan skill and reach together, rank evidence by how much it can be trusted, and never flatter.

**Use it when** the app has queued work for Claude: a critique of something you handed in, a weekly plan, a skill test to score, a research brief or library picks. Say `answer it` (or `run my queue`).

**Also answers:** `review`, `critique`, `where do I stand`, `I feel like a fraud`, and "is this worth my time?"

**What it will not do:** raise a skill level without evidence, present a blog number as fact, or promise an outcome. Plans give a range, never a guarantee.

**Needs:** the [growth](../growth) skill and the [Growth app](../../app). It installs together with `growth`.
