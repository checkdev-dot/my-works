# Library

The Library is a short, optional list of things worth your time this week. Your tasks come first. The Library feeds them and never replaces them.

## What you see

- **One best pick** at the top, labelled "Best fit this week" or "Best for tonight's task".
- **Also good**: a few more.
- **More this week**, folded: more picks, the gaps you are closing, your trinkets and what was left out.
- **Skipped**, **From earlier weeks** and **Finished**, each folded.

Each pick has a kind, a title, who made it, a length in minutes, a published date and one line of why it matters for you. Many have a "look for" line: the one thing to notice, so it turns into something you use.

| Kind | Notes |
|---|---|
| Book | An investment. It shows a price and a way to try it first. Claude suggests a book only when it is the best source for a skill you will work on for weeks. |
| Article | Under 15 minutes where possible. |
| Video | Phone-friendly. Under 20 minutes, or it says which part to watch. |
| Visual | A real product or board to study, with something to find. |
| Podcast | Phone-friendly. Scored like everything else. |
| News | Up to three things that changed lately and matter to your work, each with one small thing to try. |

## How a pick is scored

Nothing reaches the Library before it is scored. The score is Claude's judgement, with named evidence, not a popularity count. Six questions, each 0 to 10.

| Question | Weight |
|---|---|
| Fit: is it about your goal, gaps, level and this week? | 25% |
| Gain: will it change what you do? | 25% |
| Time: is the payoff worth the minutes? | 15% |
| Substance: concrete examples, or buzzwords? | 15% |
| Fresh: still true and current? | 10% |
| Best: does it win against at least two real alternatives? | 10% |

The total is 0 to 100. The app rechecks the sum.

| Total | Verdict |
|---|---|
| 80 or more | Read now |
| 65 to 79 | Worth it |
| 50 to 64 | Skim |

A pick is shown only at 65 or more, with fit, substance and fresh each at least 4, gain at least 5 and time at least 3. Anything else goes to the **left out** list with a plain reason, such as "Mostly vocabulary, no examples". That list lets you trust what you see and see what Claude passed on. A fewer-picks week, or an empty one, is a good week. Scores are not inflated to fill the list.

Picks are also checked for freshness. Anything older than 30 days is rechecked, and retired to the left out list if it no longer clears the bar.

## What you can do with a pick

- **Keep** it.
- **Skip** it, with a reason: "Not my thing", "Too long" or "Already know this". Claude uses the reason to pick better.
- **Mark it done**, with a note. A done note is evidence of learning. It never raises a skill rating on its own.

## Trinkets

A trinket is a small, checked thought from a designer or thinker that you can carry for a day. It is for delight and perspective. It is never homework and never counted. Each one rests on a page Claude opened. A short exact quote (25 words or fewer) is labelled as a quote. Anything longer is a plain-words paraphrase, labelled as one. Claude never invents a quote. There are at most six new a week.

## Refresh

Say `update my library` in Claude Code. It takes about 10 to 15 minutes. Claude checks every link and date and rebuilds the list from your gaps and your journal. It also refreshes with the Sunday review. The list does not change on its own in between.

The Library also reads your gaps. A pick names the gap it moves you toward and the task it goes with.

To judge one thing you found, say `library score <title, link or text>`. Claude scores it the same six ways and compares it with at least two alternatives. It adds nothing to the Library unless you say yes.

Related commands: `library`, `library update`, `library score`, `learn`, `news`, `recall`. See [Commands](../reference/commands.md). Next: [The Commands page](commands-page.md).
