# You

The You page is about your skills. It shows each skill, how sure the rating is and what the rating rests on.

## Levels

Skills run on one ladder, in half steps.

| Level | Word |
|---|---|
| 1 | Junior |
| 1.5 | Junior+ |
| 2 | Mid |
| 2.5 | Mid+ |
| 3 | Senior |
| 3.5 | Senior+ |
| 4 | Lead |

A plus means halfway to the next level. The page opens with your level overall and the skill that would help most.

## This month's focus

If a skill is the focus of your active skill bet, it is the hero of the page. You see:

- where you are on the ladder, and the next step,
- how far you are towards Sunday review, as a percentage (never as points),
- a button to open tonight's task for that skill, or to add evidence.

If no focus is set, the page says so. Say `plan my week` in Claude Code and Claude picks one.

## Every skill, with its evidence

Open a skill to see:

- how its rating was worked out,
- what raises it this week,
- the tasks for that skill,
- the evidence behind it, newest first,
- what the next level looks like, in the words of your rubric.

A hollow dot on the ladder means the rating is an estimate that no outside review has confirmed. Ratings from shipped work, an expert or the market show as confirmed. See [Honesty rules](../concepts/honesty-rules.md) for the trust ladder.

## How ratings move

- Claude re-rates a skill on Sunday when its bar is full, or when a higher-trust source arrives.
- A skill moves by at most half a level at a time.
- A rating with no artifact behind it is capped one level below.
- Every change adds a history entry that names the evidence.

You can ask for one rating to be checked with `rate <skill>`, or test yourself with `test <skill>`. A short scenario test gives you three to five questions. Claude scores the answers against your rubric and writes a note on each.

## What Claude knows about you

Below the skills, a "person" area lists a few things Claude has noticed, in groups such as how you learn, how you work, what gives you energy, what worries you, what you want and your limits. Nothing is kept as true until you confirm it. You can reject or edit any item, and `person clear` clears them.

## Evidence you have not linked

If journal entries are not linked to a skill, the page says how many. Open the journal to link them, so they count toward the right skill.

The A to B gap map is on [Hiring](hiring.md#the-gap-map).

Related commands: `skills`, `rate`, `test`, `gaps`, `person`. See [Commands](../reference/commands.md). Next: [Hiring](hiring.md).
