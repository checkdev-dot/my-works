#### [Font engine](apps/webapps/font-engine)
*Working name.* Shape a letter until it looks right, and it finds the real free fonts closest to it. Double-click a letter to open it, drag its own edges, pick how it is built (a `b` with or without its notch, a one- or two-storey `a`), zoom out and the whole word follows. Then keep two or three fonts and see them in seven real templates, from a dashboard to a packaging label, before you choose. An engine has measured 1,715 free font families so a shape made by hand can be compared with them directly.

### Android apps

#### [Split](apps/android/split-app)
An Android app that splits shared spending the moment you pay. It reads your bank's payment texts on the phone, asks if a payment was shared, and splits it with the same people as last time in one tap. Groups ← Home → People pages, settle up, WhatsApp reminders, and sync with friends without a server. It never moves money.

### Career app

#### [Growth](apps/growth)
A personal career growth app you run with Claude Code. It interviews you, maps your skills against the roles you want, plans your weeks, builds an honest CV where every fact has a source, and shows it all in a calm local web app. **G mini** uses the same engine to interview someone you are helping and hand them a deep PDF report. The app and its three Claude skills live together in [`apps/growth`](apps/growth), with one installer.
